Presenter

Dion Walton is a Undergraduate Student at the University of Alberta currently pursuing a BSc in Computer Engineering. He has worked since May of 2026 at ANCL as a robotics engineer and lab technician, aiding in the development and experiments motion/force control of a fully-actuated hexarotor and motion control of quadrotor with an off-center slung load. In this time, he has gained extensive experience using ROS2 and PX4 for drone control, simulation, and experimental design. His current interests are in embedded control, as well as ethical applications of intelligent systems for drones.
